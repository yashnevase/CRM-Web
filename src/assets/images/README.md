# Website images

Place approved, optimized website photography in this folder.

Use JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF. High-quality JPG files are completely fine for the first content pass. We can optimize formats later before final deployment.

Recommended image files to add:

- `hero-healthcare-professional.jpg` - main homepage hero image. A warm, modern healthcare professional or coordinator, clean background, plenty of empty space around the subject.
- `hero-healthcare-network.jpg` - optional supporting image for healthcare network/service ecosystem visuals.
- `about-care-coordination.jpg` - team member coordinating care on a laptop or speaking with a patient/client.
- `service-diagnostics.jpg` - diagnostics, pathology lab, sample collection, or medical testing environment.
- `service-corporate-healthcare.jpg` - workplace wellness, employee health check camp, or corporate medical desk.
- `care-home-healthcare.jpg` - home visit, nurse/physio/doctor with patient, calm and respectful.
- `care-corporate-program.jpg` - employee health program, medical camp, or health screening setup.
- `care-occupational-health.jpg` - workplace/industrial health assessment or doctor with employee safety context.
- `technology-dashboard.jpg` - optional abstract dashboard, operations screen, or healthcare coordination workspace.
- `network-partners.jpg` - healthcare partner network, hospital/clinic collaboration, or service provider meeting.
- `cta-healthcare-support.jpg` - friendly closing image for contact/partnership section if we decide to add one later.

Composition notes:

- Prefer bright clinical spaces, real people, natural expressions, and uncluttered backgrounds.
- Avoid obviously stock-looking handshakes, overly dark images, AI-looking faces, or fake medical claims.
- Keep the subject centered or slightly right aligned for hero images so text and floating cards can sit comfortably.

To use an image, import it in the relevant React section and pass it to `MediaPlaceholder` through the optional `src` prop.
